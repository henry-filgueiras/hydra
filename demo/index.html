Playground — primes & toy RSA, live

The same engine doing real work: generate primes and round-trip a message through textbook RSA, right here. Educational toy — Hydra is variable-time; never use this for real keys.

Results depend on your device, browser, and background load — medians of the chosen sample count per cell, warmup excluded, operand conversion outside the timed region. Correctness is cross-checked before timing (all three backends must produce identical results). Reproduce natively or rebuild this page from the hydra repo: scripts/wasm_bench.sh, scripts/wasm_demo.sh. Hydra is variable-time and for public inputs — verification workloads, not secret keys.
